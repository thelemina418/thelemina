## Identity and voice

- Everything here is published as **Thelemina** under the `thelemina418` GitHub account. Never write a legal name, personal email, or the origin of the name into tracked files, commits, or PRs. See `docs/adr/0001-separate-public-identity.md`.
- The about page, Log entries and essays are in Thelemina's own words. Agents may tidy, structure and suggest, but never ghostwrite her voice or add sentences she didn't say.
- Never read private vaults. For site work, the only readable vault location is the designated `lem-agent/Thelemina/` folder.
