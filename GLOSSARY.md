# Thelemina

The personal site of Thelemina (thelemina.dev): a public garden of everything she makes (code, music, writing, video, web art), where unfinished work is a legitimate, visible state.

## Language

**Thelemina**:
The public persona, the name everything is signed with, and the site itself. "Lem" and "Mina" are its short forms. Separate from any legal name and from the professional portfolio.
_Avoid_: brand, author account

**Garden**:
The whole collection of Thelemina's Works, in every State. What the site is.
_Avoid_: portfolio, archive, collection, projects

**Work**:
Any single thing Thelemina makes and tends in public: an app, a game, a song, an essay, a web art piece. The site's primary unit.
_Avoid_: project, post, item, portfolio piece

**State**:
Where a Work is in its life. One of **Seed**, **Growing**, **Living**, **On ice**, **Fallow**, **Finished**. Changing a Work's State is a public event.
_Avoid_: status, phase, progress

**Seed**:
A Work that has been named and begun but has little substance yet.

**Growing**:
A Work under active, visible development.

**Living**:
A Work that is out in the world and usable, and still tended: software that's released but still changing. Code's equivalent of completion.
_Avoid_: launched, live, released, maintained

**On ice**:
A paused Work that Thelemina fully intends to return to once she has the bandwidth. Always carries a **Waiting on** note.
_Avoid_: paused, blocked, backlog

**Waiting on**:
The visible note on an On ice Work naming what it's waiting for. When the note stops being true, the Work moves to Growing or Fallow.

**Fallow**:
A Work Thelemina has set down and moved on from for now, with no promise to return. Never deleted; it can grow again.
_Avoid_: abandoned, archived, dead

**Finished**:
A Work Thelemina has declared complete.
_Avoid_: done, shipped, released

**Medium**:
The kind of making a Work belongs to (code, music, writing, video, art). A facet for filtering, never the site's primary structure.
_Avoid_: category, section, type

**Log entry**:
A short, dated note: the smallest unit of publishing. Usually attached to a Work, and the default way the site gets updated.
_Avoid_: post, update, devlog, status

**Deipnon**:
The monthly review at the dark moon (Hekate's Supper), where Thelemina walks the Works, moves their States honestly, and publishes one Log entry about it.
_Avoid_: retro, review, monthly update

**Evening log**:
The end-of-day practice of turning what Thelemina chooses to share from her day into Log entries, which she approves before they're published.
_Avoid_: daily sync, import
